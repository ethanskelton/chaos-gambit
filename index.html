How it works

Pieces move like chess on a full board, but with no castling or en passant. There is no check: you win by capturing the enemy king. Pawns that reach the far row become queens.

On your turn, either move a piece or play one card. You draw a card every second turn. Upgrades bend the rules for one side for the whole game.
